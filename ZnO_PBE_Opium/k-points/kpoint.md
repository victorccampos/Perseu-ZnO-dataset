ZnO has a hexagonal structure with different lattice parameters: a ≈ 3.2 Å, c ≈ 5.2 Å

The reciprocal lattice vectors scale inversely with real-space lattice parameters

Since c/a ≈ 1.6, the Brillouin zone is shorter along the c-axis

A mesh of 8×8×6 gives roughly equivalent k-point density in all directions:

a-direction: 8 points over ~3.2 Å

c-direction: 6 points over ~5.2 Å

This gives similar point spacing (~0.4 Å⁻¹) in all directions

For higher accuracy, you might use 12×12×8, but 8×8×6 is a good balance for semiconductors
